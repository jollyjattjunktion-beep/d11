Automatic fixture updates

The match centre periodically checks the fixture provider and stores match information in the Preet Fantasy AI database. Upcoming and live matches remain visible. When a match is reported as completed or cancelled, or is no longer included in the provider's current fixture list, it is hidden from the public homepage. Its stored record is retained so imported player and scorecard history is not deleted.

Fantasy cricket analysis

Preet Fantasy AI also includes a separate team-building workspace with player roles, credits, selection trends, stadium information and rule-checked team combinations. Any fantasy estimates depend on the historical records available for a particular match and format. They are analytical estimates, not guaranteed outcomes or official match broadcasts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the cricket fixture list come from?

Public fixtures are imported from the connected Team Generation cricket fixture feed. Team names, competition labels, match timings and available team images are displayed from that source.

How often are upcoming matches updated?

The application checks the source feed periodically while the service is running and refreshes the list when someone opens the match centre after the refresh interval.

Why does a completed match disappear?

Completed and cancelled fixtures are hidden from the public upcoming/live list. The underlying match record remains in SQL so historical scorecards, player records and analysis can still refer to it.

Does Preet Fantasy AI provide official live scores or a broadcast?

No. The homepage is a fixture and match-information display. It is not an official broadcaster, and match status depends on the connected fixture provider.
