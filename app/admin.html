MATCH ANALYSIS
Automatic mode uses AI11 pitch notes and the imported match venue report. “Both” creates separate batting-first and chasing portfolios. Without toss data, select the batting-first team.
Historical fantasy point coverage will be checked at generation time. AI11 batting, bowling, series and selection data improve provisional selection ranking but are not fantasy-point projections.
Strongest mode uses a performance-first ranking, caps all-rounders at 3 and targets at least 4 specialist bowlers. Other strategies keep broader role limits. Saved picks are only forced when you enable the lock option.
